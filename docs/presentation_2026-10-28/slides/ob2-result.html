The assessor never sees fault labels; it learns each indicator’s normal range from clean training windows. It rarely flags a clean sensor and catches hard failures almost always, including the withheld stuck and clipping faults. Wrong flags on the other, healthy sensor stay at the clean rate. The weakness is vibration noise, spikes and gain, explained on the next slide.
