How OB1 was tested

Unseen bearings only

P1: every test bearing is absent from training. P3 also holds out one operating condition.

Six models, same data

Vibration, current, early, average, stacked and per-window gated fusion. Logistic experts, identical training rows and augmentation.

Sensor faults

Trained with noise, dropout, intermittent loss, gain. Tested also on withheld clipping, spikes, stuck values.

Metrics: Macro-F1, calibration (ECE), healthy-window false alarms, misses; paired Wilcoxon with Holm correction.

Protocol unchanged from the approved methodology. The key point is that every score is on bearings the model never saw, which is why numbers are much lower than the high accuracies often reported. The gate weights the vibration and current experts per window from label-free signal-quality indicators and expert confidence.
