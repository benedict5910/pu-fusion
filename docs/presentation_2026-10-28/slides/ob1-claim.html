What we can claim

Under withheld sensor faults, the gate scores higher than early fusion (+0.049) and average fusion (+0.057). It does not beat vibration-only (−0.015), and no difference is statistically significant after Holm correction.

Consistent with Kaya & Jobani (2026): on unseen Paderborn bearings, fusion gave no gain over vibration alone.

Say this sentence exactly. It is the honest headline. The gate does what it was designed for relative to naive fusion when a sensor fails in a way never seen in training, but it does not yet beat the simplest strong baseline, vibration alone, and with 11 overlapping runs nothing is significant.
