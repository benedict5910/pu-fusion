# Diagnostic audit of the starter-subset results (21 Oct 2026)

Read-only diagnostics using the same P1 folds, seeds and settings as the reported results. No model
parameter was changed and nothing was tuned on held-out bearings. Script:
`scripts/audit_diagnostics.py`; outputs: `results/starter/audit/`.

## 1. Healthy-window false-alarm rate (~0.45 in P1)

**Where it comes from: classification (generalisation to unseen healthy bearings). It is not an
evaluation error, and the health rules do not create it.**

| Held-out healthy bearing | FAR vib-only | FAR early | FAR average | FAR gated | Gated predicts it as |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K001 | 0.006 | 0.001 | 0.009 | 0.062 | healthy 94 % |
| K002 | 0.748 | 0.758 | 0.761 | 0.752 | outer race 70 % |
| K003 | 0.000 | 0.063 | 0.009 | 0.008 | healthy 99 % |
| K004 | 0.821 | 0.807 | 0.817 | 0.869 | outer 41 % / inner 46 % |

- **Concentrated in two bearings.** The window FAR is concentrated in two of the four healthy
  bearings, for every model. The pooled ~0.45 is the window-weighted mean over the folds in which
  K002 or K004 is held out (K004 is in the test set of 6 of the 11 runs, K002 of 5).
- **Depends on which healthy bearings were in training.** Each fold trained on only 2-3 healthy
  bearings. Folds that trained on both K002 and K004 had a near-zero FAR (0.002-0.003). Folds whose
  healthy training bearings were all from {K001, K003} plus one other gave the highest FAR
  (0.78-0.95). Healthy bearings evidently differ from one another as much as healthy differs from
  damaged, and 2-3 healthy training bearings do not cover that variation.
- **Fact sheets suggest no simple cause.** Run-in histories differ (K001 >50 h at 1000-3000 N,
  1500-2000 rpm; K002 19 h; K003 1 h; K004 5 h; all at 3000 N), but this does not line up cleanly
  with the two groups, so no cause is claimed.
- **Evaluation is correct.** FAR is defined as the share of healthy windows predicted non-healthy
  (`metrics.classification`). It was checked against per-window predictions: the per-bearing
  values above recombine to the reported fold FARs.
- **Health rules propagate the problem.** Replaying each held-out healthy recording (8 windows)
  through the WARNING/INSPECT rules gives a final INSPECT on 35-39 % of healthy recordings for every
  model. The rules turn persistent misclassification into alarms, as designed; they cannot fix it.
- **Operating condition.** FAR is lower at 900 rpm (N09: 0.24-0.33) than at the three 1500 rpm
  conditions (0.47-0.50).
- **Implication.** The full dataset adds K005 and K006, giving 4-5 healthy training bearings per P1
  fold. Whether that lowers FAR is a hypothesis to test after 28 Oct, not a claim.

## 2. Why vibration noise and spikes are rarely flagged by the quality assessor (OB2)

**Where it comes from: label-free bounds learned from clean training windows are wide for
vibration, because clean vibration itself varies strongly between bearings and conditions.**

| Vibration condition | flatness (median) | out of bounds | spike_db (median) | out of bounds | log_rms out of bounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| clean | 0.415 | 0.000 | 10.16 | 0.000 | 0.011 |
| noise 20 dB | 0.424 | 0.000 | 10.11 | 0.000 | 0.013 |
| noise 0 dB | 0.783 | 0.000 | 8.34 | 0.000 | 0.069 |
| noise -5 dB | 0.915 | 0.000 | 7.23 | 0.000 | 0.135 |
| spikes 10/s | 0.429 | 0.000 | 11.50 | 0.000 | 0.013 |
| spikes 1000/s | 0.841 | 0.000 | 12.53 | 0.000 | 0.096 |

- **Noise.** It raises spectral flatness strongly (0.42 to 0.92), but the learned upper bound is
  1.20, above the maximum attainable value of about 1. Per-bearing clean medians already span
  0.10-0.93, and the 25 % padding of `ReliabilityAssessor` pushes the bound past the ceiling. The
  flatness check therefore cannot fire on vibration.
- **Spikes.** Injected spikes raise `spike_db` only from 10.2 to 12.5 dB, against a learned bound
  of 19.6 dB. Damaged bearings are naturally impulsive (per-bearing clean medians 8.4-13.6 dB), so
  the clean range already contains large peaks.
- **What does fire.** Only the amplitude indicator (`log_rms`) catches anything, and only at the
  strongest settings (7-14 %).
- **Why current is unaffected.** Current is a near-pure sinusoid with little natural variation,
  so the same faults leave its normal range and are detected 100 % of the time.
- **Interpretation.** For vibration, broadband energy and impulses are both fault signatures and
  sensor-fault signatures. A purely label-free, per-indicator range cannot separate them. This is
  a substantive OB2 finding and is reported as such.
- **Possible remedies, after 28 Oct.** Condition-specific bounds, or padding chosen by
  cross-validation on training bearings only. Either must be selected without the held-out
  bearings.
