Why these OB1 numbers are preliminary

11 of 15 folds ran

4 folds were skipped by the pre-set rule: with 12 bearings, their test set lacked a class.

Small training sets

7–9 training bearings per fold, about 20 in the full design (no artificial-damage bearings yet).

Runs are not independent

All 11 runs reuse the same 12 bearings, so Wilcoxon p-values overstate the evidence.

Low power

Run-to-run sd 0.16–0.18 exceeds every model gap; smallest Holm-adjusted p for the gate is 0.117.

Fold-by-fold list: docs/P1_RUNS.md

Be upfront about these four points before anyone asks. The skip rule was in the code before any result existed; we did not drop folds after seeing results. Because the runs share bearings, the effective sample is about 12 bearings, not 11 independent runs. With this little data, only a difference that holds in essentially every run could reach significance after correction.
