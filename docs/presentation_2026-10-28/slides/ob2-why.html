Clean vibration varies widely between bearings (flatness 0.10–0.93, spike level 8.4–13.6 dB). Damaged bearings are impulsive and broadband, so these sensor faults look like bearing faults. Current is a near-pure sinusoid: caught 100 %.

Implementation issue identified: the 25 % range padding puts the flatness bound at 1.20, above its maximum of about 1, so this check can never fire. Logged; any fix will be chosen on training bearings only.

This is a genuine finding, not a bug. In vibration, broadband energy and impulses are both what bearing damage looks like and what a noisy or spiking sensor looks like, so a label-free range cannot separate them. Possible next step after this review: ranges per operating condition, or padding chosen by cross-validation on training bearings only. Neither will be tuned on the test bearings.
