# OB4 high-fidelity CAD and 3D viewer upgrade (21 Nov 2026)

Branch `ob4-cad-testbed`. One commit on top of `OB4: CAD-supported virtual testbed`.
Scope: the representative drivetrain CAD model, its FreeCAD -> STEP -> GLB pipeline and the dashboard's 3D viewer.
The diagnostic backend, the frozen models, the replay chain and the historical results are unchanged.

## What is still true (read first)

* The assembly is **representative**. It is not a reconstruction of the Paderborn bearing bench or the MCC5-THU
  gearbox bench, which are two different rigs. Dimensions are engineering choices.
* The 3D view is **not a physics model or a physics-validated digital twin**. Health colours, sensor quality,
  probabilities and fusion weights come from the frozen models' replay output (`testbed_view.cad3d_args`); the viewer
  only displays them.
* The supplied reference image was used as a visual target only. No value, state or dimension was taken from it.

## 1. Geometry (src/pufusion/drivetrain_cad.py)

Detail is fused into the solid it belongs to, so every part stays one solid and the part names, components, sensor
mounts, explode offsets and the S1-S4 data mapping keep their meaning. One part was added (`motor_fan_cowl`):
90 parts + 4 sensors = 94 solids (was 89 + 4).

| Component | Added detail |
|---|---|
| Motor | 26 axial cooling fins (clear of the terminal box and feet), fan cowl with 57-hole ventilation grille, terminal-box seat, lid with screws, cable gland and blanking plug, lifting eye, bearing cap with bolts, 6 end-shield bolts, 4 hold-down bolts on the riser |
| Couplings | flanged jaw hubs (neck + flange) with 6 bolts per hub; elastomer spider; overall length and diameter unchanged |
| Line shaft | locking collar either side of each bearing, two set screws per collar (symmetric, so the shaft stays coaxial) |
| Bearing housings | bore bosses both faces with 4 cap bolts each, 4 cast gussets, 4 hold-down bolts with washers, grease nipple; S1 on the test-bearing housing top as before |
| Gearbox | split-line flange, foot flange with 4 bolts, 7 vertical ribs (the -y wall keeps S4's mounting area flat), open bearing bosses and blind caps on both end walls with 4 bolts each and a rib below each, cover with inspection cover, breather, two lifting eyes and 6 cover bolts, plinth with bolted base flange |
| Gears | involute teeth (20 deg pressure angle, 0.1 mm backlash, polygonal) replacing the trapezoidal form; hubs; 6 lightening holes in the wheel |
| Load unit | 14 axial fins, end bells with 6 bolts, rear cap, terminal box, cradle saddle on a bolted pedestal |
| Base plate | anchor nuts at the 4 holes, 6 levelling feet with pads |
| Sensors | S1, S3, S4 drawn as cylindrical accelerometers with the same bounding boxes and centres as before (sensor positions unchanged) |

New primitive: `prism` (polygon section extruded along an axis) for fins, ribs, gussets and hexagon heads; its
position follows `Params`, its section is fixed. FreeCAD builds it as a `Part::Feature` with bound placement.
Each part carries a `finish` class (painted, painted_dark, plate, cast, machined, black_oxide, elastomer,
bearing_steel, gear, cable, painted_load) used only by the viewer's materials.

## 4. Viewer (app/cad3d_component/, plain WebGL, no new dependencies)

* Physically based shading: GGX specular + Lambert, metalness/roughness per finish, analytic studio environment
  (gradient sky and soft boxes) for metal reflections, brushed variation on machined shafts, ACES tone mapping,
  linear colour workflow with sRGB output.
* Key-light shadow map (2048, depth packed into RGBA8 so it works on WebGL 1 and 2; 5x5 PCF soft edges) on the
  parts and on a floor that fades out radially; ground occlusion term; fill and rim lights; MSAA.
* Health states from the replay: restrained tint plus state-coloured rim and edges on the monitored component
  (green / amber / red / purple from `ui.STATE_COLOR`); cyan only for a user selection.
* Callouts on leader lines with overlap avoidance; labels kept clear of the toolbar, title and inspector;
  inactive sensors lean aside.
* Inspector: subsystem and dataset, health state, probabilities, sensor channels, quality, score and fusion weight,
  all from the frame.
* Camera: exact perspective fit of every solid into the area not covered by the inspector and legend; the fitted
  view re-fits when the frame is resized until the user moves the camera; double-click focus scales its margin with
  part size; stored camera key bumped (v4) so the new default framing applies.

## 5. Playback fix found while testing

Switching Bearing -> Gearbox -> Bearing previously discarded the replay (the view kept one session and rebuilt it
on every subsystem change). `testbed_view.render` now keeps one session per (subsystem, recording) and remembers
each subsystem's recording; switching pauses the running replay and resumes it on return. Verified on the live
dashboard (bearing window 3 -> gearbox window 4 -> bearing still 3, Step -> 4 -> gearbox still 4) and covered by
the new AppTest `test_switching_subsystem_keeps_each_replay`.

## 6. Known limits

* Fins, ribs and bolt heads are fused into their part and drawn in that part's finish (bolts are not separately
  coloured zinc). Threads, keys, seals, raceway grooves and cages are not modelled.
* The gear teeth are a polygonal involute for display and clearance checking only.
* Dashboard screenshots were captured from the desktop app's browser pane at 800x500; a full-resolution render of
  the same viewer and GLB (headless Chrome, sample frame, not a replay) is included for detail.
* The healthy-K004 false WARNING and the gearbox model limitations documented in `OB4_TESTBED.md` are unchanged.

Screenshots: `results/ob4_cad/screenshots_hifi/` (index in its README).
