We audited the false-alarm rate rather than adjusting thresholds. Folds that trained on both K002 and K004 had almost no false alarms (0.002–0.003). Folds whose healthy training bearings came only from the other group reached 0.78–0.95. So healthy bearings differ from each other as much as from damaged ones, and two or three healthy training bearings are not enough. The full dataset adds K005 and K006; whether that helps is a hypothesis to test, not a claim. We did not tune anything on the test bearings.
