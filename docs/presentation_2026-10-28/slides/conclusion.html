Conclusion

OB1: on unseen bearings, fusion is no better than vibration alone; the gate helps against early and average fusion only under withheld sensor faults, not significantly.

OB2: hard sensor failures are caught reliably; vibration noise and spikes are not, for a diagnosed reason.

OB3: the health layer and dashboard work end to end on real data.

OB4: code ready; hardware not started.

Main open problem: false alarms on unseen healthy bearings, likely from too little healthy-bearing diversity in training.

Close honestly: one objective complete, two evaluated with partly negative results, one pending. The leakage-safe protocol is what makes these numbers credible, even though they are lower than many published figures.
